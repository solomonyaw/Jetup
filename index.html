Digital Financial Ecosystem

What is JetUP?

JetUP is a digital financial ecosystem that brings together trading infrastructure, copy trading, AI-powered trading strategies and a partner/referral program. The ecosystem connects users with TAG Markets and its CopyX platform, where users can access trading strategies such as Sonic AI. JetUP also provides an IB Portal for managing partner-related activities and referral information. Users may participate as traders, copy trading strategies through CopyX, or as partners who refer new participants and potentially earn commissions from qualifying activities within their network.

Where Does Your Money Go?

When funding your account, you deposit capital directly into your own personal brokerage account at TAG Markets via supported cryptocurrencies (USDT, etc.).

Due Diligence & Safety

Comprehensive Risk Categories

Independent educational overview of primary risk factors associated with margin trading, copy execution, and broker custody.

Market Volatility Risk

Forex and CFD assets experience sharp fluctuations triggered by geopolitical events, macroeconomic reports, or unexpected liquidity changes.

CATEGORY 01

Leverage Multiplier Risk

Leverage magnifies downside losses equally with upside profits. A minor adverse price movement can deplete your margin rapidly.

CATEGORY 02

Strategy Drawdown Risk

Automated strategies undergo losing trades and periods of drawdown. Historical performance metrics never guarantee future success.

CATEGORY 03

Platform & Slippage Risk

Server latency, API disconnections, or market gap slippage can impact order execution pricing between master and follower accounts.

CATEGORY 04

Crypto Funding Risk

Cryptocurrency transfers are irreversible. Network gas fees, blockchain confirmations, or misdirected addresses can result in capital loss.

CATEGORY 05

Capital Loss Potential

You may lose some or all of your deposited funds. Only commit surplus capital that you can afford to lose without endangering your living needs.
